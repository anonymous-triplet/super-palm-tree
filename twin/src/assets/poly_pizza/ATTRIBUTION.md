# External city model attribution

The active city scenes use externally authored GLB assets downloaded from Poly Pizza. Individual assets are either CC0 or CC-BY as listed by Poly Pizza; this complete attribution is retained with the project and distributable archive.

- [City Pack](https://poly.pizza/bundle/City-Pack-q11onRvPoJ) by [dreamdev](https://poly.pizza/u/dreamdev) [CC-BY] via Poly Pizza.
- [City Pack](https://poly.pizza/bundle/City-Pack-kJqRAIGsw0) by [J-Toastie](https://poly.pizza/u/J-Toastie) [CC-BY] via Poly Pizza.

The bundled packs include creator-authored buildings, cars, buses, people, trees, traffic signals, hydrants, power boxes, and street furniture. The city preserves the source GLB files rather than converting them into locally generated substitutes.
